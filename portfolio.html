Christian Howell: Casa delle Foglie


The English word portfolio comes from the Italian portafoglio, which in turn derives from portare, meaning "to carry" and foglio meaning "leaf" or "sheet." A portfolio, therefore, refers to the leaves that one carries around. As I do not carry my leaves around with me, but house them here, it seems to make more sense to call this my house of leaves, or my Casa delle Foglie.


EAGLE ML

 | 

Applied Research Lab at Penn State

At Penn State's ARL, I worked with a small team to develop a novel approach to implementing time difference of arrival (TDOA) geolocation techniques with deep learning to reduce the amount of time required for estimating the source location of a signal. This involved creating millions of points of synthetic signal data, performing TDOA on each of the points, and training a neural network to predict the original geographic location of the signal.

Self-Driving Intersection Approach

 | 

Argonne National Lab

The biggest energy inefficiency when driving a car occurs when we press the brake, converting kinetic energy into thermal energy. In EVs, some of that energy can then be used for regenerative braking. At Argonne, we looked at a fleet of self-driving electric cars to find the most efficient braking trajectory (i.e., how should we slow down at an interesection to maximize the amount of energy recovered via regenerative braking?).

Recipe Recommender

Netflix, Spotify, and many of society's other favorite media platforms use matrix factorization to store information using less data and, more importantly, to recommend shows, songs, and other products based on how similar users rated those products. I had a friend training for an IRONMAN 70.3, and we decided to use matrix factorization to build a recipe recommender for personal use so he could get proper nutrition as he was training.

Magnetic Reconnection

 | 

NASA Space Apps

Magnetic reconnection events occur when magnetic field lines break and reconnect—hence the name. When this happens, energy is released which can lead to solar flares and auroras forming. I participated in the NASA Space Apps with some friends, and we built a tool to use NASA's OMNIWeb data to search for when these events happened in the past.

Music Score Follower

 | 

ACME, Brigham Young University

While musical score following for melodies of individually played notes can be quite accurate, when the music becomes polyphonic or multi-instrumental the task of reliably following a score becomes much more challenging. Working with some classmates in BYU's applied mathematics program, we used Kalman Filters, Hidden Markov Models, and variations of the Viterbi algortithm to tackle this problem.
