I'm Christian. My job takes up a good chunk of my day each day, and while I am there, I am sorted into the "software engineer" or "machine learning engineer" bucket. At one point, a job and a title meant something to me. When I was in college I told everyone that I was a student and that I was studying math. People cared (or at least pretended to care) about that, and that is what they would ask me about. When I graduated, the question changed from "What are you studying?" to "What are you doing?" or "Where are you working?" Both are valid questions, but increasingly, I have come to understand the cliché that we are not what we do. So, while professionally I may fit neatly into the "software engineer" bucket, with experience with Python, C#, SQL, Git, Bash scripting, and building using AI tools like Copilot and Claude Code, I am personally more interested in what I am doing outside of work.
