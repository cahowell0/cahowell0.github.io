Christian Howell: Japanese Journey


Japanese

Just after high school, from 2017 to 2019, I spent close to two years in Japan. That was the first time that I started studying a foreign language. While studying math at BYU, I made sure to always fit at least one Japanese class into my schedule each semester and I lived in the Language Immersion Student Residence on campus where all of my roommates and apartment mates spoke in our target language. Eventually, I ended up graduating with a minor in the Japanese.

I never expected that minor to be useful, but in the fall of 2025 I was told that a nearby university was looking for someone who could take on a role as an adjunct Japanese instructor. Jokingly, I asked if I could. I spent the next two semesters teaching Japanese to second language learners, and I really loved it. Teaching Japanese to college students reminded me how much I enjoy learning and talking about the language. Since then, I have renewed my commitment to study and learn Japanese, and as a goal, I would like to be able to pass the JLPT at the N1 level and, hopefully, the Kanken at level 1 (or at least level 3).

Japanese Bucket List:

  • Kanken Level 5
  • Kanken Level 4
  • Kanken Level 3
  • JLPT N2
  • JLPT N1
