Christian Howell: Triathlon

I started riding bicycles as a way to get around while I was living in Japan back in 2017. I continued using bikes as my main mode of transportation throughout college. Even now, as much as is practical, I try to get where I am going by bicycle and train.

I did cross country in high school, mostly because I thought it would look good on college applications. Now I regret that I didn't put more time and effort into it. I'm not as fast or strong now as I was in high school, but I am getting back into it.

Swimming is tough. I want to be able to do it, but it's tough. For now I am aiming to be able to swim the distance needed to participate in a sprint triathlon.
