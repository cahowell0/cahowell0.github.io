Christian Howell: Photography


Photography

I am not a photographer. But Momo and I got a mirrorless camera not too long ago and I've been having fun taking pictures with it. Now all kinds of photography seem interesting to me—street photography, wildlife photography, landscape photography, portrait photography. Focusing on all types all at once might be a little bit much, and since I don't really have too many people asking for portraits, I figured I'd try my hand at wildlife photography. I'm still figuring things out, so we'll see how it goes.
